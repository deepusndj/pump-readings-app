Privacy policy

Mangolino Uploader is a private tool for the owner of the Mangolino YouTube channel. Last updated: 11 November 2026.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it uses the YouTube API to upload videos to the owner's own channel and to read that channel's videos and statistics.

How the information is used and stored

The Google sign-in credentials (an access permission token) are stored only on the owner's own computer. Channel information is used only to show the owner their own statistics and to perform the uploads the owner asks for.

Sharing

No Google user data is sold, shared with, or disclosed to anyone else. It is not used for advertising.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
